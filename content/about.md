I’m a systems and infrastructure professional who enjoys making technology reliable, secure, and easier to use. My work brings together Linux administration, cloud and container platforms, automation, and web development.

I’ve worked with Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL-based systems, Bash, Python, Ansible, Docker, and AWS. I’m also comfortable building responsive websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and Hugo, and I bring a visual design perspective from graphic design and UI/UX work.

I like solving practical problems, learning new tools, and seeing a project through—from understanding what people need to building and improving a solution. I’m based in Lagos, Nigeria, and open to opportunities to contribute to thoughtful teams and useful projects.
